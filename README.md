# Paisa Planner: Expense Tracker

![Paisa Planner "Plan Ring" logo](docs/logo-plan-ring.png)

Private, offline expense tracker for India. It turns your bank and UPI SMS, bank emails, receipts and quick notes into budgets, all on your phone. No internet permission, so your data never leaves your phone.

## Install

1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the APK and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap "More details" and then "Install anyway".
4. Setup asks for no permissions. You can turn on SMS auto-tracking later from Home or Settings.
5. On Android 13+, if the SMS permission is greyed out, go to Settings → Apps → Paisa Planner → ⋮ → "Allow restricted settings".
