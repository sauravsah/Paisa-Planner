# Paisa Planner: Expense Tracker

Private, offline expense tracker for India. It reads your bank and UPI SMS on your phone and turns them into budgets. No internet permission, so your data never leaves your phone.

## Download

**[Download PaisaPlanner-v3.3.apk](https://github.com/sauravsah/Paisa-Planner/raw/main/PaisaPlanner-v3.3.apk)** (about 3 MB, Android 8.0+)

## Install

1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the APK and allow "Install unknown apps" for your browser or file manager when asked.
3. If Play Protect shows a warning, tap "More details" and then "Install anyway".
4. On Android 13+, if the SMS permission is greyed out, go to Settings → Apps → Paisa Planner → ⋮ → "Allow restricted settings".

## Features

- SMS auto-tracking (OTPs ignored)
- Salary-cycle budgets and monthly plan
- "Want it? Park it." wishlist cool-off
- Subscription radar with reminders
- No-spend streaks and habits
- Lent and borrowed tracker
- Money Story monthly recap
- Import from bank emails: share an email to the app, or turn on email alerts (optional, on-device)
- Fingerprint lock, light and dark themes
